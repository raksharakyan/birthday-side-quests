# Offer Research: Global batch (non-India)

Research date: **2026-10-02**. Data file: `research/offers.batch-global.json` (52 entries: 50 new and 2 updated, `panera-us` and `costa-gb`; 49 verified, 3 unverified).

Method is the same as `docs/OFFER_RESEARCH.md`. Web search was used only to discover programs and URLs. Every offer is paraphrased from the brand's own domain. Pages that blocked WebFetch were read in the built-in browser, read-only (no sign-in, no forms), and the Notes column marks these as "(browser)". Sites that showed a bot challenge (Cloudflare, Akamai, or a "checking your browser" page) were **not** bypassed.

## Concerns

- **Tier and spend gates.** Several verified entries are not open to everyone. Crumbl (Silver+), MECCA ($300+/yr), Starbucks IE/AE (Gold only), Sephora FR (Black+), Smoothie King (free only for Champion), Clinique ($55 purchase), PizzaExpress (£15 minimum), and Cheesecake Factory and Grill'd (purchase needed). The offer text says so, but the app may want to flag these.
- **Generic rewards.** Some entries confirm a reward exists but not what it is: Target, DQ, DSW, Rita's, Zizzi, BurgerFuel, Starbucks SG, Lindt and Tealive. The wording was kept generic on purpose.
- **Denny's has probably dropped its birthday reward.** Remove `dennys-us`, or demote it to points-only.
- Some `nameRegex` values are short or generic and may over-match on OSM: `^tank`, `insomnia`, `costa`, `indigo|chapters`, `mecca`, `lindt`, and `^target$`. Scope matching by country.
- Two patterns contain `.`, which acts as a wildcard: `p.? ?f.? chang'?s` and `t.g.i. friday'?s`. They're within the allowed charset but loose.
- Wikidata IDs were added only where I'm confident (Starbucks, Nando's, Sephora, Panera and Costa match the existing draft; also Chili's, Cheesecake Factory, DQ, Target, Greggs, PizzaExpress and wagamama). Spot-check them before use.

## Removals

- **dennys-us: delete it.** The current official rewards pages (https://dennys.com/rewards and https://dennys.com/rewards-terms) describe a BoothBucks points program with no birthday reward at all.

## Claim-detail fields (added 2026-10-02)

Every entry in `offers.batch-global.json` now has `rewardItem`, `steps`, `purchaseRequired` and `validFor`. Some also have `minSpend`, `signupLeadDays` and `bring`. All of these come only from what the cited official page said when I read it (see the findings table).

- `rewardItem` is "Not published by the brand, varies by member" wherever the page gives only a generic gift, treat, voucher or surprise. That covers Corner Bakery, Rita's, DQ, Clinique, DSW, Target, PizzaExpress, Zizzi, wagamama, Space NK, Lindt, MECCA, BurgerFuel, Starbucks SG, Tealive, Sephora FR and the 3 unverified entries.
- `purchaseRequired` is set only when the page says so. It's true for Cheesecake Factory, Clinique, PizzaExpress, Grill'd and Indigo. It's false for Gelatissimo, whose terms say the scoop counts as a visit only with an extra purchase. Everything else is null.
- Prior-purchase rules are eligibility conditions, so they appear in `steps`, not in `purchaseRequired`. Examples are Nando's NZ, Max Brenner, San Churro, Insomnia and Booster Juice.
- `signupLeadDays` is set only for Booster Juice (account 7+ days old) and BurgerFuel (24 hours, recorded as 1).
- `steps` contain only actions the page supports. No app screens or button names are guessed.
